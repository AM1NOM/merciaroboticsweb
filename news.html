🏆 1st Place

We Won the Yorkshire Regional — We're Going to Nationals!

We are thrilled to announce that Mercia Robotics came 1st place at the Yorkshire Regional Competition at Sheffield Hallam University! On top of that, we were honoured with the prestigious Inspire Award, recognising our team's outstanding dedication to spreading the values of FIRST beyond the competition field.

We will now be heading to the UK Nationals in London on the 21st and 22nd of April, where we will compete against 49 other top teams from across the country. This is an incredible achievement for the whole team and we couldn't be prouder.

Thank you to our coach, Mr Rees, our sponsors, ACCU and Flagship, and everyone who has supported us on this journey. The best is yet to come!
