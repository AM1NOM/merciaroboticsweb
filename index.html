Mercia Robotics

Mercia Robotics is the FIRST Tech Challenge (FTC) team from Mercia School. Our students learn engineering, coding, and teamwork by designing competitive robots. We compete in the annual FTC challenge and run outreach sessions to inspire younger students.

Become a Sponsor Meet the Team

About Us

FIRST (For Inspiration and Recognition of Science and Technology) is a prestigious international program founded in 1989 to ignite scientific curiosity and innovation. This year, our team of 17 driven young adults will compete against over 60 teams from across the UK to design and build an autonomous robot over the course of 9 months — culminating in a high-stakes national challenge in London.

Together, we can build something extraordinary.
